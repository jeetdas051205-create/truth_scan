Key Scientific Takeaways

Observations for college presentation defense:

  • LinearSVC & Bi-LSTM: Achieved equivalent top test accuracy (98.81%), showing both linear boundary optimization and sequential embeddings capture truth signals effectively.
  • Passive Aggressive Classifier: Extremely fast training (~0.18s) with high precision (99.11%), making it ideal for high-throughput live news wire APIs.
  • Explainability Advantage: TF-IDF linear weights expose specific deceptive tokens (e.g., sensationalist adjectives) directly to end users.
Recommended Deployment: Use Support Vector Machine (LinearSVC) for ultra-low latency API responses (<25ms) with high explainability, complemented by Bi-LSTM for deep contextual validation.

TruthScan AI: Academic Project Report

Comprehensive documentation of problem statement, architecture, methodology, and empirical findings.

1 Problem Statement & Motivation

The digital era has witnessed an unprecedented proliferation of disinformation, fake news, and coordinated propaganda campaigns across social networks and news syndicates. Misinformation impairs democratic processes, creates public health crises, and induces financial market volatility.

Manual fact-checking by human journalists cannot scale to millions of articles published daily. This capstone project introduces TruthScan AI: an automated, scalable machine learning and deep learning architecture that analyzes linguistic syntax, stylistic markers, and lexical distribution to detect misinformation with 98.8% accuracy.

3 Methodology & Pipeline

  1. Corpus Assembly: Ingestion of the 34,103 ISOT dataset articles and 23,196 FakeNewsNet articles.
  2. Text Preprocessing: Regex sanitation removing URLs, HTML fragments, source tags, non-alphanumeric noise, and lowercasing.
  3. Stratified Partitioning: 80% train (27,282 articles) and 20% test (6,821 articles) maintaining exact 50/50 balance without data leakage.
  4. TF-IDF Vectorization: Unigram + bigram feature generation with sublinear term frequency scaling capped at 10,000 features.
  5. Model Benchmarking: Training and cross-evaluating 5 classical ML models + 1 deep sequence model.
  6. Production Serialization: Persistent storage using Joblib and Keras HDF5 format with sub-25ms inference.

5 Limitations & Future Scope

Limitations:

  • Linguistic models classify textual patterns and writing styles, not real-time external ground truth facts.
  • Domain transfer accuracy drops on informal social media slang and memes (as evidenced in FakeNewsNet).
  • Multimodal misinformation (manipulated video/deepfakes) is outside the scope of text NLP.

Future Scope:

  • Integration with live Google Knowledge Graph and Wikipedia API verification.
  • Multimodal image-text cross-attention using CLIP or Gemini vision models.
  • Browser extension for automated real-time social media feed scanning.

How TruthScan AI Works

End-to-end processing pipeline from raw article ingestion to calibrated confidence scoring.

1

News Article Input

User submits article text via the Web UI or via the POST /api/predict JSON REST endpoint. Text is validated for minimum length (>15 words).

2

Linguistic Sanitation & Cleaning

Regex filters eliminate HTML tags, HTTP/HTTPS links, source metadata tags, and punctuation noise. Characters are lowercased and spaces collapsed to standardize the feature space.

3

TF-IDF Vectorization & Tokenization

The pre-fitted TF-IDF vectorizer converts tokens into a 10,000-dimensional sparse matrix incorporating unigrams and bigrams with sublinear term-frequency weighting.

4

Model Inference & Probability Calibration

The selected classifier (LinearSVC, Bi-LSTM, PAC, Random Forest, etc.) executes inference. Calibrated probabilities quantify uncertainty into a defensible 0-100% confidence score.

5

Explainability & Verdict Rendering

Top positive and negative coefficient n-grams are extracted and mapped to the article text, explaining which words triggered the verdict alongside processing latency (<25ms).
